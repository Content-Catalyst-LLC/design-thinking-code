# Method Note: Contextual Inquiry and Synthesis

This folder provides a professional reproducible workflow for documenting contextual inquiry evidence and design research synthesis.

The central scoring model is:

\[
C_i = w_e E_i + w_s S_i + w_m M_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Evidence support** captures the strength and richness of evidence units connected to a theme.
- **Stakeholder coverage** captures whether the theme is supported across relevant participant groups.
- **Method triangulation** captures whether the theme appears across multiple forms of inquiry.
- **Interpretive risk** captures uncertainty, overgeneralization, sampling limitations, and potential bias.

## Recommended interpretation

Theme scores should be treated as structured prompts for team review, not as automated conclusions. A theme with high confidence may be ready to inform insight generation or prototyping. A theme with high validation priority should be reviewed before it guides major design decisions.

## Professional questions

- Which evidence units support the theme?
- Which stakeholder groups are represented?
- Which stakeholder groups are missing?
- Which research methods produced the evidence?
- What contradictory cases exist?
- What assumptions are being made?
- What would weaken this interpretation?
- What design decision does this theme support?
