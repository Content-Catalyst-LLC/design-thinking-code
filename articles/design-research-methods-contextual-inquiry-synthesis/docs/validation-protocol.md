# Validation Protocol

## Evidence validation

- [ ] Every evidence unit has a unique identifier.
- [ ] Participant groups are documented.
- [ ] Research methods are documented.
- [ ] Primary and secondary themes are assigned.
- [ ] Evidence strength and interpretive risk are scored.
- [ ] Privacy sensitivity and power asymmetry are reviewed.
- [ ] Field notes distinguish observation from interpretation.
- [ ] Sensitive data has been minimized and protected.

## Synthesis validation

- [ ] Themes are traceable to evidence units.
- [ ] Stakeholder coverage is reviewed.
- [ ] Method triangulation is reviewed.
- [ ] Contradictory cases are documented.
- [ ] High-risk themes are validated before design decisions.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are identified.
- [ ] Coder disagreement is reviewed.
- [ ] Saturation claims are limited to the sampling frame.

## Design validation

- [ ] Each theme connects to a design implication.
- [ ] Each design implication is grounded in evidence.
- [ ] High-priority themes have a prototype or research next step.
- [ ] Ethical risks are reviewed before sharing artifacts.
- [ ] Stakeholder validation is used where appropriate.
