# Method Note: Professional Design Pathway Scoring

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for design thinking work.

The model compares possible design pathways across four criteria:

\[
V_i = w_h H_i + w_f F_i + w_l L_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Human relevance** captures stakeholder need, usability, accessibility, lived experience, equity, and contextual fit.
- **Feasibility** captures technical, organizational, operational, legal, financial, and implementation practicality.
- **Learning value** captures how much a pathway can teach the team through prototyping, testing, iteration, and early deployment.
- **Residual risk** captures unresolved ethical, operational, political, technical, adoption, and unintended-consequence risks.

## Professional interpretation

The score is not a final answer. It is a structured prompt for judgment.

A strong professional design process should ask:

- Who assigned the scores?
- What evidence supports each score?
- Which stakeholders were included?
- Which stakeholders were missing?
- What assumptions are embedded in the weights?
- What evidence would change the ranking?
- Which pathway should be prototyped before implementation?
- Which risk is unacceptable even if the pathway scores well?

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- early-stage design strategy;
- service redesign prioritization;
- public-sector innovation review;
- design research synthesis;
- institutional decision workshops;
- prototype portfolio selection;
- research documentation.
