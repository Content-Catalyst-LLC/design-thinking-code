# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every concept has a unique name.
- [ ] Concept type and transition domain are documented.
- [ ] Usability, feasibility, ecological benefit, circularity, equity, durability, and risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Evidence quality is documented.
- [ ] Stakeholder coverage is documented.
- [ ] Lifecycle boundary quality is documented.
- [ ] Burden-shift risk and implementation complexity are documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Transition pathway data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Sustainability validation

- [ ] Ecological benefit claims are tied to evidence.
- [ ] Lifecycle boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Circularity claims include end-of-life, reuse, repair, or material retention logic.
- [ ] Equity and burden distribution are reviewed.
- [ ] Labor and maintenance implications are considered.
- [ ] Governance and implementation owners are identified.
- [ ] Risks of rebound, displacement, green gentrification, and burden shifting are reviewed.
- [ ] Post-launch monitoring is planned.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Evidence strength is assessed.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limits are reported.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are documented.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are considered.
- [ ] Sustainability claims are not made from adoption data alone.
