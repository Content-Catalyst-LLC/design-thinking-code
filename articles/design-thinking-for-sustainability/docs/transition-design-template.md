# Transition Design Template

## Purpose

Transition design connects long-term sustainability goals to practical steps that can be tested, evaluated, and governed.

## Core fields

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Current system | What unsustainable system pattern is being addressed? |
| Desired transition | What long-term change is sought? |
| Ecological constraint | What environmental pressure or boundary matters? |
| Stakeholders | Who is affected, who acts, and who governs? |
| Burdens | Who pays, who works, who maintains, and who is at risk? |
| Prototype | What near-term intervention can be tested? |
| Evidence | What will show that the prototype is working? |
| Governance | Who can revise, scale, pause, or stop the intervention? |
| Equity safeguards | What protections are needed? |
| Learning cadence | How often will evidence be reviewed? |
