# Learning Governance Protocol

## Purpose

Strategic learning requires governance: someone must review evidence, change decisions, and own consequences.

## Governance requirements

- Strategic owner.
- Evidence owner.
- Implementation owner.
- Ethics/public value owner.
- Review cadence.
- Evidence thresholds.
- Stop/pivot/scale rules.
- Risk escalation path.
- Stakeholder feedback loop.
- Public or internal reporting commitments.
- Revision authority.

## Key question

What evidence would change the strategy, and who has authority to act on that evidence?
