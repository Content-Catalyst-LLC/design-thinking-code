# Ethics and Power Review

## Purpose

Strategy distributes attention, resources, risk, burden, and power. Ethical strategy review makes those distributions visible.

## Review questions

- Who defines the strategic problem?
- Who benefits from the option?
- Who carries burden?
- Who is excluded?
- Who has decision power?
- What trade-offs are being hidden?
- What public value is created or weakened?
- What harms are plausible?
- How can affected stakeholders challenge or correct the strategy?
- What evidence would stop the option from scaling?

## Red flags

- Participation without influence.
- Efficiency that shifts burden.
- Growth that weakens trust.
- Automation without recourse.
- Data strategy without contestability.
- Public value claims without public accountability.
