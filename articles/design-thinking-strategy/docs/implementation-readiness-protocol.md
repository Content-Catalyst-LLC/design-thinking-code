# Implementation Readiness Protocol

## Purpose

A strategy is not real until it can be delivered.

## Readiness areas

- Staff capacity.
- Roles and ownership.
- Data availability.
- Service workflows.
- Governance and decision rights.
- Funding and resource commitments.
- Training and adoption support.
- Technical infrastructure.
- Risk and recovery processes.
- Measurement and feedback loops.

## Required question

Can the organization deliver the strategic option without relying on hidden labor, vague ownership, or unsupported frontline work?
