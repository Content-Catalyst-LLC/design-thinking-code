# Evidence Validation Protocol

## Validation checks

- [ ] Source evidence is available.
- [ ] Method is documented.
- [ ] Participant coverage is adequate for the claim.
- [ ] Missingness has been reviewed.
- [ ] AI output is source-grounded.
- [ ] Human review is complete.
- [ ] Minority and edge-case signals are preserved.
- [ ] Consent and reuse limits are respected.
- [ ] Finding is linked to a decision record.
- [ ] Uncertainty is documented.

## Decision readiness

A research signal may be ready for design decision review when confidence, traceability, validation, consent alignment, and participant coverage are strong and bias risk is low or explicitly mitigated.
