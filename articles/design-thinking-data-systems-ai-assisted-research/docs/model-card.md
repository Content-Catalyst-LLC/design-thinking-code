# Model Card: AI-Assisted Design Research Evidence Model

## Model name

AI-Assisted Design Research Evidence Scoring Model

## Intended use

The model supports structured review of research evidence before findings are used to shape design decisions. It evaluates source strength, relevance, traceability, representativeness, validation, missingness risk, AI-assistance risk, decision relevance, recency, consent alignment, participant coverage, metadata quality, and governance priority.

## Not intended for

- Certifying research findings as true.
- Replacing human analysis.
- Replacing participant or community interpretation.
- Replacing privacy, legal, accessibility, or ethics review.
- Automatically deciding which design direction should be selected.
- Treating AI-generated summaries as validated evidence.

## Responsible use

Use the model to identify where stronger source review, validation, consent review, participant coverage, bias analysis, or AI governance is required.
