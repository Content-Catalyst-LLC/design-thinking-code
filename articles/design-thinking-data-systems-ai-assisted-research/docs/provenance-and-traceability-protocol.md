# Provenance and Traceability Protocol

## Purpose

Design research claims should be traceable from finding to source to decision.

## Evidence-to-decision chain

1. Raw source.
2. Method record.
3. Consent and reuse constraints.
4. Metadata record.
5. Coding or tagging record.
6. AI-use record, if applicable.
7. Human validation.
8. Finding or research signal.
9. Design decision or recommendation.
10. Post-decision outcome or learning record.

## Minimum traceability rule

A research finding should not be used in design decision-making unless the team can identify its source evidence, method, date, participant group or data source, limitations, review status, and decision relevance.
