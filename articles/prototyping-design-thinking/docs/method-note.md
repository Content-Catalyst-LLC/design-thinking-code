# Method Note: Prototype Portfolio Evaluation

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for professional prototyping in design thinking.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_l L_i + w_f F_i + w_u U_i + w_e E_i + w_m M_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Learning gain** captures how much the prototype can teach the team.
- **Feasibility signal** captures whether the prototype can reveal build, workflow, staffing, or operational feasibility.
- **User response** captures observed or expected stakeholder reaction.
- **Equity value** captures whether the prototype helps evaluate access, burden, inclusion, or differential impact.
- **Implementation relevance** captures how strongly the prototype informs real next-step decisions.
- **Composite risk** captures ethical, operational, technical, and scaling risk.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- prototype portfolio review;
- concept comparison;
- next-round prototype selection;
- risk review;
- ethical and accessibility review;
- pilot readiness assessment;
- stakeholder discussion;
- design decision documentation.

## Interpretation

The model is not a final answer. It makes assumptions visible. A prototype that scores highly may still require ethical safeguards, accessibility testing, stakeholder validation, or operational review before it advances.
