# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every intervention has a unique name.
- [ ] Intervention type and evaluation stage are documented.
- [ ] Outcome, burden, equity, trust, durability, cost, and risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Evidence quality is documented.
- [ ] Stakeholder coverage is documented.
- [ ] Method triangulation is documented.
- [ ] Uncertainty is documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Outcome time-series data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Evaluation validation

- [ ] Theory of change is documented.
- [ ] Learning questions are explicit.
- [ ] Outputs are distinguished from outcomes.
- [ ] Baseline or comparison conditions are documented.
- [ ] Subgroup and equity analysis is planned.
- [ ] Burden is measured across users, staff, caregivers, and intermediaries where relevant.
- [ ] Qualitative evidence is reviewed alongside quantitative indicators.
- [ ] Unintended consequences are monitored.
- [ ] Findings are connected to decision rights.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Evidence strength is assessed.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limits are reported.
- [ ] Missing groups are documented.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are considered.
- [ ] Causal claims are only made when the evaluation design supports them.
