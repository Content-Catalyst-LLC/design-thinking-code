# Learning Agenda Template

## Purpose

A learning agenda defines what the design team needs to learn, what evidence will answer those questions, and what decisions depend on the evidence.

## Core questions

1. What outcome is the intervention intended to improve?
2. What burden is the intervention intended to reduce?
3. Which groups may experience different outcomes?
4. What risks or unintended consequences must be monitored?
5. What evidence is strong enough to justify scaling?
6. What evidence would require revision, pause, or retirement?
7. Who has authority to act on the findings?

## Suggested table

| Learning question | Claim type | Primary metric | Secondary metric | Evidence source | Review cadence | Decision use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Did the intervention improve the intended outcome? | Outcome | Outcome score | Current quality | Administrative data | Quarterly | Continue, revise, or stop |
| Did burden decrease? | Burden | Burden score | Staff burden | Journey data and staff logs | Monthly | Redesign support or workflow |
| Did equity gaps narrow? | Equity | Equity gap | Subgroup completion | Equity monitoring | Monthly | Adapt or target support |
| Did trust improve? | Trust | Trust score | Complaint themes | Surveys and interviews | Quarterly | Adjust transparency |
