# Method Note: Design Evaluation, Learning, and Outcome Measurement

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for evaluating design interventions after testing, implementation, or rollout.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_o O_i + w_b B_i + w_e E_i + w_t T_i + w_d D_i - w_p P_i
\]

Where:

- **Outcome improvement** captures whether the intervention improved the intended condition.
- **Burden reduction** captures whether user, staff, caregiver, or intermediary burden decreased.
- **Equity performance** captures whether benefits and burdens are distributed fairly.
- **Trust improvement** captures confidence, legitimacy, privacy comfort, and reliance.
- **Durability** captures whether improvements persist over time and across routine conditions.
- **Penalty** combines operational cost and residual risk.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- outcome evaluation;
- post-launch learning review;
- service-quality assessment;
- equity and burden analysis;
- evidence-strength assessment;
- learning agenda review;
- intervention portfolio comparison;
- scale, revise, pause, or retire decisions.

## Interpretation

A high evaluation score is not proof of causal impact. It should be interpreted alongside evidence quality, stakeholder coverage, method triangulation, uncertainty, subgroup results, qualitative evidence, and governance context.
