# Evaluation Governance Protocol

## Purpose

Evaluation governance ensures that evidence is interpreted responsibly and connected to decisions.

## Roles

- **Evaluation owner:** accountable for evidence quality and reporting.
- **Design owner:** accountable for translating findings into design revisions.
- **Service owner:** accountable for operational decisions and support changes.
- **Equity owner:** accountable for differential outcomes and burden.
- **Data steward:** accountable for data quality, privacy, and documentation.
- **Decision sponsor:** accountable for scale, pause, revise, or retire decisions.

## Review cadence

- Monthly issue and burden review.
- Monthly equity and access review.
- Quarterly outcome review.
- Quarterly learning agenda review.
- Post-rollout unintended-consequence review.
- Annual intervention continuation or retirement review.

## Decision options

- Continue as designed.
- Continue with safeguards.
- Revise the intervention.
- Add support capacity.
- Run additional evaluation.
- Narrow scope.
- Pause rollout.
- Retire the intervention.
