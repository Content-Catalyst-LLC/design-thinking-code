# Model Card: Behavioral Design Intervention Priority Model

## Intended use

The model supports transparent evaluation of behavioral barriers and candidate interventions.

## Not intended for

- Automated behavioral targeting.
- Dark-pattern design.
- Manipulative conversion optimization.
- High-stakes eligibility, employment, financial, health, or public-benefit decisions.
- Individual-level surveillance or profiling.
- Replacing ethical review, community review, accessibility testing, or field experimentation.

## Responsible use

Use the model to support deliberation, not manipulation. Treat high ethical risk, low transparency, weak autonomy preservation, low accessibility effect, or unequal equity reach as reasons for redesign before testing or scaling.
