# Method Note: Testing and Validation Decision Support

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for testing and validation in design thinking.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_d D_i + w_f F_i + w_v Vi_i + w_s S_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Desirability** captures stakeholder meaning, usefulness, and perceived value.
- **Feasibility** captures technical, operational, staffing, and workflow feasibility.
- **Viability** captures sustainability across cost, governance, policy, and maintenance.
- **Responsibility** captures safety, equity, privacy, transparency, and ethical fit.
- **Combined risk** captures observed friction and residual uncertainty.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- prototype-test review;
- validation scenario comparison;
- concept advancement decisions;
- stop, revise, pivot, or advance decisions;
- risk review;
- accessibility and equity review;
- pilot readiness assessment;
- stakeholder discussion;
- evidence documentation.

## Interpretation

A high score is not implementation approval. A tested concept may still require further accessibility review, equity testing, field validation, governance review, legal review, technical review, or operational piloting.
