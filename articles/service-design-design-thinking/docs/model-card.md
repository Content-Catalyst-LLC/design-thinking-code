# Model Card: Service Design Journey Model

## Model name

Service Design Journey Quality and Reliability Model

## Intended use

The model supports transparent evaluation of service stages across completion probability, clarity, trust, accessibility, user burden, staff load, recovery quality, frontstage quality, backstage readiness, policy complexity, and data dependency.

## Not intended for

- Automated service decisions.
- Replacement of user research, staff research, accessibility testing, or service blueprinting.
- Certifying service quality without stakeholder review.
- Evaluating individual staff performance.
- Public-benefit eligibility or allocation decisions.
- Algorithmic triage without governance review.

## Inputs

- Service stage.
- Stage order.
- Channel.
- Completion probability.
- Clarity.
- Trust.
- Accessibility.
- User burden.
- Staff load.
- Recovery quality.
- Frontstage quality.
- Backstage readiness.
- Policy complexity.
- Data dependency.
- Optional user-group data.
- Optional service-blueprint dependency data.
- Optional service risk register.

## Outputs

- Service-stage quality.
- End-to-end service reliability.
- Failure risk.
- Burden risk.
- Operational friction index.
- Procedural dignity index.
- Redesign priority.
- Service resilience.
- User-group equity gaps.
- Blueprint friction index.
- Risk-register priority.
- Monte Carlo uncertainty outputs.
- Bootstrap stability outputs.
- Random-weight sensitivity outputs.

## Responsible use

Use the model to support deliberation and service learning. Treat weak reliability, high burden, low accessibility, high equity gaps, high blueprint friction, or unstable rankings as reasons for deeper research and service redesign.
