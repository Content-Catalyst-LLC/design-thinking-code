# Service Governance Protocol

## Purpose

Service governance ensures that service design does not stop at journey maps, blueprints, or prototypes.

## Governance requirements

- End-to-end service owner.
- Stage owners.
- Channel owners.
- Data owners.
- Policy owners.
- Accessibility owner.
- Recovery owner.
- Service quality review cadence.
- Incident and complaint review.
- Service metrics dashboard.
- User and staff feedback loop.
- Revision authority.
- Implementation funding.

## Key question

Who has authority to improve the whole service when problems cross organizational boundaries?
