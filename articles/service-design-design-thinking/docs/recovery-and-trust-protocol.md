# Recovery and Trust Protocol

## Purpose

Service recovery design protects dignity and trust when a service fails.

## Recovery requirements

- Clear explanation of what went wrong.
- Continuity of case context.
- Human escalation when needed.
- Correction pathway.
- Appeal or review pathway.
- Status visibility.
- Time expectation.
- Apology or acknowledgement where appropriate.
- Ownership of resolution.
- Feedback loop after resolution.

## Trust indicators

- Users understand what happened.
- Users know what to do next.
- Users can reach support.
- Users do not have to restart unnecessarily.
- Errors can be corrected.
- Decisions can be challenged.
- Staff have authority to help.
