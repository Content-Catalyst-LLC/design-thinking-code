# Accessibility and Burden Protocol

## Purpose

Accessibility and burden review helps ensure that a service is practically usable, not merely formally available.

## Accessibility review

- Language access.
- Disability access.
- Device and connectivity access.
- Human or assisted support.
- Plain-language content.
- Alternative channels.
- Time and scheduling flexibility.
- Compatibility with assistive technologies.

## Burden review

- Time burden.
- Documentation burden.
- Cognitive burden.
- Emotional burden.
- Coordination burden.
- Repeat-contact burden.
- Status-checking burden.
- Appeal or correction burden.

## Required question

Who must do extra work to make the service function, and is that work fairly distributed?
