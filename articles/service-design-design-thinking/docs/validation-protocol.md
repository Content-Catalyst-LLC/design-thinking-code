# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every service stage has a unique name.
- [ ] Stage order is documented.
- [ ] Completion probabilities are in the interval (0, 1].
- [ ] Service scores use a documented 1-10 scale.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] User-group data is traceable.
- [ ] Blueprint dependency data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Service validation

- [ ] Journey stages are validated with users.
- [ ] Blueprint layers are validated with staff.
- [ ] Accessibility is tested with affected groups.
- [ ] Non-users and abandoners are included where possible.
- [ ] Staff workload and hidden labor are measured.
- [ ] Recovery pathways are tested.
- [ ] Equity gaps are disaggregated.
- [ ] Governance owners are assigned.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Service claims are not based on satisfaction alone.
- [ ] Reliability and burden are assessed across the full journey.
- [ ] Average improvements are checked against group-level harms.
- [ ] Service recovery is evaluated.
- [ ] Implementation conditions are documented.
