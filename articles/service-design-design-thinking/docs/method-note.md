# Method Note: Service Design in Design Thinking

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for evaluating service journeys, service blueprints, user burden, staff load, accessibility, trust, recovery quality, and operational readiness.

The central model is:

\[
Q_i = w_pP_i + w_cC_i + w_tT_i + w_aA_i + w_rR_i - w_bB_i - w_sS_i
\]

Where:

- **Completion probability** captures the likelihood that users can complete a service stage.
- **Clarity** captures legibility of instructions, status, rules, and next steps.
- **Trust** captures perceived reliability, fairness, transparency, and credibility.
- **Accessibility** captures whether people with different abilities, languages, devices, and contexts can use the service.
- **Recovery quality** captures how well the service responds when things go wrong.
- **User burden** captures time, cognitive effort, documentation, stress, and coordination work.
- **Staff load** captures delivery effort, hidden labor, exception handling, and operational strain.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- service journey analysis;
- service blueprint review;
- public service redesign;
- customer experience redesign;
- employee service design;
- digital service transformation;
- AI-assisted service governance;
- accessibility and burden review;
- service recovery planning;
- service measurement and evaluation.

## Interpretation

A high redesign-priority score is not proof that a stage should be redesigned in one specific way. It indicates that the stage deserves deeper research, blueprinting, operational review, accessibility testing, and stakeholder participation.
