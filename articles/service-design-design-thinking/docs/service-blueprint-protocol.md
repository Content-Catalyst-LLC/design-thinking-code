# Service Blueprint Protocol

## Purpose

Service blueprinting connects visible user experience to the backstage roles, systems, policies, and data that make a service possible.

## Recommended blueprint layers

1. User actions.
2. Physical or digital evidence.
3. Frontstage touchpoints.
4. Frontstage staff actions.
5. Backstage staff actions.
6. Support processes.
7. Data systems.
8. Policy rules.
9. Handoffs.
10. Failure and recovery paths.
11. Governance owners.
12. Quality and learning loops.

## Diagnostic questions

- Which backstage dependencies determine visible service quality?
- Where does handoff risk appear?
- Where do users carry coordination work?
- Where do frontline staff absorb hidden labor?
- Which systems or data dependencies are fragile?
- Which rules create unnecessary burden?
- Who owns the end-to-end service?
