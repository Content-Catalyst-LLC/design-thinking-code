# Ethics Risk Review Protocol

## Purpose

Ethical risk review identifies harm severity, probability, exposure, detectability, accountability, repairability, privacy risk, autonomy risk, and manipulation risk.

## Required fields

- Design decision.
- Affected groups.
- Harm description.
- Severity.
- Probability.
- Exposure.
- Detectability.
- Accountability.
- Repairability.
- Governance owner.
- Mitigation status.
- Stop, pause, redesign, or scale rule.

## Stop conditions

Pause or redesign when:

- harm severity is high;
- exposure is broad;
- detectability is weak;
- accountability is weak;
- repairability is weak;
- affected groups have low voice;
- the decision creates privacy, autonomy, due-process, or manipulation risk.
