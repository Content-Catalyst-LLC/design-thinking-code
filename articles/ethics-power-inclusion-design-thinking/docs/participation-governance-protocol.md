# Participation Governance Protocol

## Purpose

Participation should be described honestly. Consultation, participation, co-design, co-production, and community control are not interchangeable.

## Review questions

- Who defines the design problem?
- Who controls research questions?
- Who interprets evidence?
- Who prioritizes design directions?
- Who controls resources?
- Who can stop or redirect the work?
- Who receives compensation?
- Who receives findings back?
- Who governs post-launch repair?

## Red flags

- Calling consultation co-design.
- Asking for lived expertise without compensation.
- Inviting participation after the decision is already made.
- Extracting stories of harm without repair commitments.
- Excluding high-burden groups because they are harder to reach.
