# Repair and Accountability Protocol

## Purpose

Ethical design thinking requires repair pathways before harm occurs.

## Repair requirements

- Clear complaint and reporting channel.
- Error correction process.
- Appeal or review process where decisions affect access or rights.
- Human support path.
- Documentation of harm.
- Timely response commitments.
- Public or internal accountability owner.
- Remediation plan.
- Post-repair monitoring.

## Key distinction

Iteration improves a design. Repair addresses responsibility to people harmed by the design.
