# Accessibility Review Protocol

## Purpose

Accessibility must be designed from the beginning of research, participation, prototyping, testing, and implementation.

## Review areas

- Screen reader compatibility.
- Keyboard and switch access.
- Captioning and transcripts.
- Plain language.
- Multilingual support.
- Cognitive accessibility.
- Assisted-access pathways.
- Offline alternatives.
- Flexible participation channels.
- Caregiver and intermediary support.
- Error recovery and appeal.

## Key requirement

Do not treat digital completion rates as evidence of accessibility unless disabled users and people with access needs are included in testing and review.
