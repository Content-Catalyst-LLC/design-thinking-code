# Model Card: Ethics, Power, and Inclusion Review Model

## Model name

Ethics, Power, and Inclusion Review Model

## Intended use

The model supports structured review of design decisions, stakeholder burden, inclusion, power gaps, participation influence, tokenism risk, governance need, ethical risk, repairability, privacy sensitivity, autonomy risk, and manipulation risk.

## Not intended for

- Automating ethical approval.
- Replacing community governance.
- Replacing disability access review.
- Replacing legal or privacy review.
- Certifying a design as ethical.
- Ranking communities or individuals.
- Using ethical scores as performative proof of responsibility.

## Responsible use

Use this model to identify where deeper review, participation, redesign, governance, or repair is required. A low score does not prove safety. A high score indicates the need for stronger scrutiny.
