# Method Note: Ethics, Power, and Inclusion in Design Thinking

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for evaluating inclusion, burden, power, participation, ethical risk, accessibility, accountability, repairability, privacy sensitivity, autonomy risk, and manipulation risk in design-thinking projects.

## Core models

Inclusion:

\[
I_g = w_aA_g + w_vV_g + w_sS_g + w_cC_g + w_rR_g + w_kK_g
\]

Burden:

\[
B_g = \alpha T_g + \beta C_g + \gamma E_g + \delta D_g + \lambda U_g + \theta O_g
\]

Ethical risk:

\[
R = H \times P \times X \times (1-D) \times (1-A)
\]

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- ethical design review;
- accessibility review;
- participatory design governance;
- public service design review;
- AI and data design review;
- inclusion and burden audits;
- community accountability planning;
- repair and recourse planning.

## Interpretation

Scores support deliberation. They do not replace affected-community leadership, accessibility expertise, legal review, public accountability, or ethical judgment.
