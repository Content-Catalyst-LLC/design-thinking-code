# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every pilot has a unique name.
- [ ] Policy domain and pilot type are documented.
- [ ] Accessibility, feasibility, legitimacy, equity, burden reduction, durability, and risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Evidence quality is documented.
- [ ] Stakeholder coverage is documented.
- [ ] Participation quality is documented.
- [ ] Legal complexity and implementation complexity are documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Policy-learning pathway data is traceable.
- [ ] Administrative burden data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Policy-design validation

- [ ] Legal authority is reviewed.
- [ ] Public participation is meaningful and documented.
- [ ] Administrative burdens are measured across citizens, staff, and community partners.
- [ ] Equity and access risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Digital exclusion risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Due process and appeal access are reviewed.
- [ ] Privacy and surveillance risks are reviewed.
- [ ] Implementation capacity is tested.
- [ ] Policy feedback and revision authority are assigned.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Evidence strength is assessed.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limits are reported.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are documented.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are considered.
- [ ] Public-value claims are not made from uptake data alone.
