# Policy Feedback Template

## Purpose

Policy feedback connects implementation evidence to revision authority.

## Core fields

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Policy intent | What public value is the policy meant to create? |
| Target population | Who is affected and who is eligible? |
| Access pathway | How do people learn about, access, use, and maintain the policy? |
| Administrative burden | What learning, compliance, psychological, digital, time, and appeal burdens exist? |
| Equity risk | Which groups may be excluded, overburdened, monitored, or harmed? |
| Implementation owner | Who can revise the policy or service? |
| Evidence signals | What will show access, burden, equity, legitimacy, or implementation failure? |
| Revision trigger | What evidence requires a change? |
| Pause trigger | What harm requires stopping or slowing implementation? |
| Learning cadence | How often will evidence be reviewed? |
