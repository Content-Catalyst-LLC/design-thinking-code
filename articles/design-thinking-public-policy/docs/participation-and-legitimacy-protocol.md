# Participation and Legitimacy Protocol

## Purpose

Participation and legitimacy review helps distinguish meaningful democratic design from symbolic consultation.

## Required questions

1. Who was invited to participate?
2. Who was missing?
3. Which high-burden groups were specifically included?
4. Were participants compensated when appropriate?
5. Was participation accessible by language, disability, time, technology, transportation, and safety?
6. What decision authority did participants have?
7. How was input translated into design or policy change?
8. What feedback was returned to participants?
9. What public justification exists for the final decision?
10. How will legitimacy be monitored after implementation?

## Legitimacy dimensions

| Dimension | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Transparency | Clear explanation of goals, options, constraints, and decisions. |
| Contestability | Appeals, objections, review, and correction mechanisms. |
| Representation | Inclusion of affected communities and marginalized groups. |
| Accountability | Identified owners, commitments, monitoring, and reporting. |
| Trust | Public confidence, institutional history, and credible intermediaries. |
| Fairness | Distribution of benefits, burdens, risks, and procedural protections. |
