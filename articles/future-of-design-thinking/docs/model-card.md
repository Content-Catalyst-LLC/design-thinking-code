# Model Card: Future Design-Thinking Readiness Model

This model supports structured review of future-facing design initiatives. It does not replace ethics review, AI governance, community participation, legal review, funding authority, implementation ownership, or institutional stewardship.

## Intended use

- Strategic portfolio review.
- AI governance readiness review.
- Stewardship planning.
- Evidence and learning infrastructure planning.
- Public-value and systems-design capability assessment.
