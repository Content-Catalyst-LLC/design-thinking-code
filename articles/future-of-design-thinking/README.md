# The Future of Design Thinking

This folder contains professional companion code and reproducible research assets for the article **“The Future of Design Thinking.”**

The article frames the future of design thinking as a shift from workshop-centered innovation toward systems learning, responsible AI, evidence infrastructure, ethics, public value, implementation capacity, governance, and long-term stewardship.

Repository URL:

https://github.com/Content-Catalyst-LLC/design-thinking-code/tree/main/articles/future-of-design-thinking

## Professional use case

This scaffold is designed for design strategy teams, public-sector innovation teams, AI governance groups, service design teams, civic technology programs, institutional learning teams, sustainability initiatives, social impact teams, design educators, research operations teams, and organizational change leaders.

It supports future design-readiness scoring, systemic design portfolio analysis, AI-governance maturity review, evidence and learning infrastructure analysis, ethics and power review, stewardship-readiness scoring, public-value prioritization, unmanaged-risk assessment, institutional absorption analysis, scenario-weighted portfolio review, Monte Carlo uncertainty simulation, bootstrap stability analysis, random-weight sensitivity analysis, SQL-backed design-futures schemas, and reproducible reports.
