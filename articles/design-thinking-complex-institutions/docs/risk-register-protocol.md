# Institutional Risk Register Protocol

## Purpose

Institutional design options should include risk registers before piloting or scaling.

## Required risk fields

- Risk ID.
- Design option.
- Risk category.
- Risk description.
- Severity.
- Likelihood.
- Detectability.
- Repair difficulty.
- Mitigation owner.
- Mitigation status.

## Stop conditions

Pause or redesign when risk is high and mitigation ownership, repair pathway, policy authority, or community accountability is weak.
