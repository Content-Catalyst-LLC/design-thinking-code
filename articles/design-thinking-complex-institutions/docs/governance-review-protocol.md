# Governance Review Protocol

## Purpose

Institutional design work must be connected to authority, budget, decision rights, policy ownership, data governance, implementation ownership, community accountability, escalation, review cadence, and maintenance.

## Review questions

- Who owns the institutional problem?
- Who can approve the change?
- Who controls the budget?
- Who controls the policy interpretation?
- Who controls the data system?
- Who owns implementation?
- Who can pause or stop the work?
- Who represents community accountability?
- Who maintains the design after launch?
- How will evidence change decisions?

## Red flags

- No named owner.
- No budget pathway.
- No maintenance owner.
- Community input without influence.
- Pilot without scale authority.
- AI/data changes without data governance.
- Evaluation findings without decision authority.
