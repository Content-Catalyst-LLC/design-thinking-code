# Institutional Burden Audit Protocol

## Purpose

Institutional improvements can shift burden from the institution to people served, frontline staff, caregivers, advocates, or partner organizations. A burden audit makes this visible.

## Burden dimensions

- Time.
- Cognitive load.
- Emotional stress.
- Documentation.
- Uncertainty.
- Coordination.
- Accessibility.
- Translation or language access.
- Travel or channel burden.
- Appeal and repair burden.

## Required disaggregation

Analyze burden by stakeholder group, language, disability, geography, income, age, digital access, and trust where appropriate.
