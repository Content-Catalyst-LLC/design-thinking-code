# Method Note: Design Thinking and Systems Thinking

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for comparing design-system interventions.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_h H_i + w_s S_i + w_f F_i + w_e E_i + w_d D_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Human-centered value** captures usability, comprehension, access, trust, dignity, and burden reduction.
- **Systemic leverage** captures the degree to which an intervention may alter deeper structures, feedback loops, rules, information flows, or governance.
- **Feasibility** captures implementability under operational and institutional conditions.
- **Equity sensitivity** captures whether the intervention is attentive to differential access, burden, trust, and outcome distribution.
- **Durability** captures whether the intervention can persist under routine conditions.
- **Risk** captures implementation risk, unintended consequences, burden shifting, power asymmetry, and system drift.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- system intervention portfolio review;
- leverage-mapping sessions;
- design-system strategy;
- service-system redesign;
- policy-delivery design;
- public-sector innovation;
- sustainability interventions;
- organizational transformation;
- implementation planning;
- evaluation and learning agenda design.

## Interpretation

A high score is not proof of systemic impact. It should be interpreted alongside evidence quality, stakeholder coverage, system maps, qualitative evidence, governance review, implementation capacity, and post-launch learning.
