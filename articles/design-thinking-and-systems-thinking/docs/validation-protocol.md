# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every intervention has a unique name.
- [ ] Intervention type and leverage level are documented.
- [ ] Human value, system leverage, feasibility, equity sensitivity, durability, and risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Evidence quality and stakeholder coverage are documented.
- [ ] Context complexity, delay sensitivity, and burden-shift risk are documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Feedback dynamics data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Design-system validation

- [ ] Lived experience evidence is linked to system structure.
- [ ] System boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Leverage hypotheses are documented.
- [ ] Feedback loops and delays are mapped.
- [ ] Burden-shift risks are assessed.
- [ ] Equity and power implications are reviewed.
- [ ] Implementation feasibility is tested.
- [ ] Evaluation measures include touchpoint, service, operational, and system-level outcomes.
- [ ] Post-launch learning loops are assigned to decision owners.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Evidence strength is assessed.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limits are reported.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are documented.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are considered.
- [ ] System-level claims are not made from touchpoint-only evidence.
