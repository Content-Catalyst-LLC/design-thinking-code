# Leverage Mapping Protocol

## Purpose

Leverage mapping helps teams determine whether an intervention acts on symptoms, workflows, information flows, rules, governance, or deeper system purpose.

## Leverage levels

1. **Parameter change:** adjusts a visible feature, message, threshold, or metric.
2. **Workflow change:** changes steps, handoffs, roles, or touchpoints.
3. **Information-flow change:** changes what information is visible, timely, trusted, or actionable.
4. **Feedback-loop change:** changes how the system learns and responds.
5. **Rule change:** changes eligibility, procedures, standards, permissions, or constraints.
6. **Governance change:** changes authority, accountability, review, or decision rights.
7. **Infrastructure change:** changes the capacities that make future action possible.
8. **Purpose or paradigm change:** changes what the system is trying to optimize.

## Review questions

- What leverage level does this intervention target?
- What evidence supports that claim?
- What burden might shift?
- What feedback might be delayed?
- Who gains or loses authority?
- What unintended consequences are plausible?
- What would show that the intervention affected system behavior rather than only local experience?
