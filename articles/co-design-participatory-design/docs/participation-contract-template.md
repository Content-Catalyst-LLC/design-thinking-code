# Participation Contract Template

## Purpose

A participation contract makes expectations explicit before co-design begins.

## Fields

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Why is participation being requested? |
| Scope | What decisions are open to influence? |
| Constraints | What legal, technical, funding, safety, or governance constraints exist? |
| Participant role | What will participants do? |
| Influence rights | How can participants shape framing, synthesis, prototypes, decisions, or governance? |
| Compensation | How will participant time and expertise be recognized? |
| Data use | What will be collected, stored, shared, anonymized, or deleted? |
| Risks | What risks or discomforts may arise? |
| Feedback loop | How will participants learn what happened after participation? |
| Accountability | Who owns implementation and follow-up? |
