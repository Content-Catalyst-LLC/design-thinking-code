# Facilitation and Accessibility Protocol

## Facilitation standards

- Clarify what is open for influence and what is fixed.
- Explain participation rights, constraints, and decision pathways.
- Protect lower-power participants from institutional intimidation.
- Use structured activities that prevent domination by high-status voices.
- Offer anonymous contribution pathways where needed.
- Treat disagreement as evidence, not disruption.
- Preserve dissent and unresolved tensions.
- Report back after synthesis and decisions.

## Accessibility standards

- Provide plain-language materials.
- Provide language support where needed.
- Provide disability accommodations.
- Offer flexible timing and format.
- Consider transportation, childcare, connectivity, and device access.
- Compensate participant time when appropriate.
- Avoid methods that require unnecessary disclosure of trauma or personal harm.
- Provide contact points and clear next steps.
