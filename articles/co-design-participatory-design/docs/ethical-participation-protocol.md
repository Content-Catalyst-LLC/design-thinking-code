# Ethical Participation Protocol

## Purpose

Ethical participation review helps prevent co-design from becoming tokenistic, extractive, inaccessible, or disconnected from decision authority.

## Core questions

1. Who invited participation and why?
2. Who defined the problem before participation began?
3. Who is most affected?
4. Which affected groups are missing?
5. What influence do participants have?
6. Are participants compensated or otherwise supported?
7. What access barriers exist?
8. What data is collected, stored, and shared?
9. How will participants know what changed?
10. Who has authority to implement, revise, pause, or reject outcomes?

## Review domains

| Domain | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Representation | Recruitment scope, missing groups, non-users, edge cases, affected publics. |
| Accessibility | Language, disability access, timing, location, transport, childcare, digital access. |
| Influence | Role in framing, synthesis, concept generation, testing, implementation, governance. |
| Compensation | Payment, support, reciprocity, attribution, and benefit sharing. |
| Data governance | Consent, minimization, confidentiality, deletion, and reuse limits. |
| Accountability | Feedback loops, decision rationale, implementation owner, and follow-up commitments. |
