# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every activity has a unique name.
- [ ] Activity type and design stage are documented.
- [ ] Representation, accessibility, influence, trust, evidence, accountability, decision impact, and ethical risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Affectedness weights are documented.
- [ ] Compensation quality and feedback-loop quality are documented.
- [ ] Tokenism risk is documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Participant-group data is traceable.
- [ ] Prototype-learning data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Participation validation

- [ ] Participants know what is open for influence.
- [ ] Affected groups are represented or missing groups are documented.
- [ ] Access supports are provided and evaluated.
- [ ] Participants are compensated where appropriate.
- [ ] Data use and consent are clear.
- [ ] Participants help validate synthesis where feasible.
- [ ] Decision changes are traceable to participation.
- [ ] Feedback loops are closed.
- [ ] Implementation owners are assigned.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Participation claims are not based on attendance alone.
- [ ] Influence is assessed across design stages.
- [ ] Ethical and tokenism risks are reported.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are documented.
- [ ] Dissent and unresolved tensions are preserved.
