# Participatory Synthesis Protocol

## Purpose

Participatory synthesis prevents design teams from collecting participant evidence and then interpreting it entirely behind closed doors.

## Recommended practices

1. Preserve participant language where appropriate.
2. Document how themes were created.
3. Invite participants to review themes and problem frames.
4. Identify contradictions and dissent.
5. Separate frequency from severity.
6. Track whose evidence is missing.
7. Identify institutional assumptions challenged by participants.
8. Connect synthesis to design decisions.
9. Report which participant contributions changed the design.
10. Explain why some contributions could not be acted on.

## Synthesis outputs

- Theme map.
- Evidence traceability table.
- Participant review notes.
- Decision impact log.
- Dissent and unresolved tension log.
- Implementation commitments.
