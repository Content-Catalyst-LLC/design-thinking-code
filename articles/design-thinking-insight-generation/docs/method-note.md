# Method Note: Professional Insight Scoring

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for insight generation in design thinking.

The model compares candidate insights across four criteria:

\[
V_i = w_p P_i + w_e E_i + w_o O_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Pattern support** captures whether the candidate insight is supported across observations, methods, contexts, or stakeholder groups.
- **Explanatory depth** captures whether the insight explains why a pattern matters rather than merely naming a theme.
- **Opportunity value** captures whether the insight opens meaningful downstream design questions, prototype hypotheses, or problem-frame refinements.
- **Interpretive risk** captures overgeneralization, sampling bias, confirmation bias, evidence thinness, solution capture, and premature closure.

A more granular risk index may be represented as:

\[
R_i = \lambda_S S_i + \lambda_B B_i + \lambda_T T_i + \lambda_L L_i
\]

Where:

- \(S_i\) = sampling risk;
- \(B_i\) = confirmation-bias risk;
- \(T_i\) = evidence-thinness risk;
- \(L_i\) = solution-capture risk.

## Professional interpretation

The score is not a final answer. It is a structured prompt for design-research judgment.

A strong insight-generation process should ask:

- What observations support this candidate insight?
- Which stakeholder groups contributed evidence?
- Which methods produced the evidence?
- What alternative explanations could account for the same pattern?
- What contradictory cases exist?
- What kind of design opportunity does the insight open?
- What would make the insight weaker?
- What prototype or research test could validate it?

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- qualitative synthesis;
- insight statement review;
- evidence tagging;
- opportunity translation;
- prototype prioritization;
- research planning;
- bias review;
- decision documentation.
