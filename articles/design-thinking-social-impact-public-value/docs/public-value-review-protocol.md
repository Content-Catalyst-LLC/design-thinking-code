# Public Value Review Protocol

## Purpose

A social impact intervention should be reviewed for public value before piloting, scaling, or institutionalizing.

## Review dimensions

- Access.
- Equity.
- Dignity.
- Legitimacy.
- Accountability.
- Outcome strength.
- Sustainability.
- Learning capacity.
- Community-defined value.
- Repair capacity.
- Governance strength.
- Stewardship capacity.

## Required questions

- Who defines public value?
- Who is affected?
- Who carries burden?
- Who can influence decisions?
- Who can challenge errors?
- Who receives repair?
- What evidence would justify scaling?
- What evidence would require stopping or redesign?
