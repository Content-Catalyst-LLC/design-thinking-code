# Social Impact Risk Register Protocol

## Purpose

Social impact interventions should include risk registers before piloting or scaling.

## Required fields

- Risk ID.
- Intervention.
- Risk category.
- Risk description.
- Severity.
- Likelihood.
- Detectability.
- Repair difficulty.
- Mitigation owner.
- Mitigation status.

## Stop conditions

Pause or redesign when risks are high and mitigation ownership, repair pathways, community accountability, or governance authority are weak.
