# Participation Protocol

## Purpose

Participation should be named honestly and designed with accessibility, compensation, decision influence, feedback loops, and power sharing.

## Participation levels

- Inform.
- Consult.
- Test.
- Co-design.
- Co-govern.
- Community-led.

## Minimum requirements

- Clear purpose.
- Accessible participation format.
- Compensation or support where appropriate.
- Language access.
- Feedback loop.
- Documentation of how participation changed decisions.
- Explicit statement of decision influence.
- Ethical review for high-stakes work.
