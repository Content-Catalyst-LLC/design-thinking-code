# Social Impact Burden Audit Protocol

## Purpose

A social impact intervention should reduce avoidable burden rather than shift it to communities, families, frontline workers, or nonprofits.

## Burden dimensions

- Time.
- Cognitive load.
- Emotional stress.
- Documentation.
- Uncertainty.
- Coordination.
- Accessibility.
- Transportation.
- Language access.
- Appeal and repair effort.

## Review rules

- Measure baseline and post-intervention burden.
- Disaggregate by group.
- Track repair access.
- Track power and voice.
- Treat small-group harms as important even when average results improve.
