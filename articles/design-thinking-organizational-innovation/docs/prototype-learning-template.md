# Prototype Learning Template

## Purpose

Prototype learning connects design experiments to organizational decision-making.

## Fields

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| Concept | What is being tested? |
| Assumption | What belief must be true for the concept to work? |
| Stakeholders | Who must use, trust, operate, or govern the concept? |
| Prototype format | What tangible version will be tested? |
| Learning metric | What evidence will show whether the assumption holds? |
| Qualitative evidence | What observations, interviews, or field notes are needed? |
| Quantitative evidence | What rates, times, errors, adoption, burden, or performance indicators are needed? |
| Equity check | Who may be excluded or overburdened? |
| Friction check | What organizational friction may block adoption? |
| Decision trigger | What evidence leads to continue, revise, scale, pause, or stop? |
