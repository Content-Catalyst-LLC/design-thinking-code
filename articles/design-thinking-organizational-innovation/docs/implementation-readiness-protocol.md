# Implementation Readiness Protocol

## Purpose

Implementation readiness review prevents organizations from mistaking a promising prototype for a scalable operating model.

## Required questions

1. Who owns the concept after launch?
2. Who funds adoption, maintenance, support, and evaluation?
3. Which systems or data dependencies must work?
4. Which teams must change behavior?
5. What training and support are needed?
6. What metrics will change?
7. What risks must be monitored?
8. What evidence triggers revision or rollback?
9. What would count as unacceptable burden or harm?
10. How will learning continue after implementation?

## Readiness dimensions

| Dimension | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Named accountable owner and decision rights. |
| Funding | Budget for launch, support, maintenance, and evaluation. |
| Capability | Staff, skills, systems, and workflows. |
| Governance | Review cadence, escalation, and accountability. |
| Adoption | Communication, training, incentives, and change support. |
| Ethics | Equity, accessibility, privacy, labor, and burden review. |
