# Method Note: Design Thinking and Organizational Innovation

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for comparing organizational innovation concepts across design, strategic, ethical, learning, implementation, and risk dimensions.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_d D_i + w_f F_i + w_v V_i + w_e E_i + w_l L_i + w_m M_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Desirability** captures stakeholder value, usability, adoption potential, and meaningful need.
- **Feasibility** captures practical, technical, operational, and organizational feasibility.
- **Viability** captures strategic, financial, and institutional viability.
- **Equity** captures ethical adequacy, accessibility, inclusion, and burden distribution.
- **Learning value** captures how much the concept can teach the organization through prototyping.
- **Implementation readiness** captures whether the organization can absorb the change.
- **Risk** captures unintended consequences, ethical harm, operational risk, and adoption failure.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- innovation portfolio review;
- design strategy;
- service and workflow redesign;
- internal transformation;
- AI-assisted workflow governance;
- employee experience design;
- customer recovery design;
- knowledge-system redesign;
- implementation planning;
- prototype and pilot review;
- responsible innovation governance.

## Interpretation

A high score is not proof that a concept should be implemented. It should be interpreted alongside qualitative research, frontline participation, technical review, accessibility review, privacy review, labor implications, governance capacity, ethical assessment, and implementation evidence.
