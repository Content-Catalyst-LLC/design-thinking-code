# Validation Protocol

## Data validation

- [ ] Every concept has a unique name.
- [ ] Concept type and organizational domain are documented.
- [ ] Desirability, feasibility, viability, equity, learning value, implementation readiness, and risk scores are documented.
- [ ] Evidence quality is documented.
- [ ] Stakeholder coverage is documented.
- [ ] Technical complexity, organizational complexity, and ethical risk are documented.
- [ ] Scenario weights sum to 1.0.
- [ ] Prototype-learning data is traceable.
- [ ] Organizational-friction data is traceable.
- [ ] Risk-register entries have owners and mitigation status.

## Organizational validation

- [ ] Stakeholders and affected groups are represented.
- [ ] Frontline labor and workload are reviewed.
- [ ] Accessibility and inclusion are tested.
- [ ] AI or automation risks are reviewed when relevant.
- [ ] Privacy, surveillance, and data-use concerns are reviewed.
- [ ] Decision authority and ownership are assigned.
- [ ] Implementation capacity is tested.
- [ ] Learning cadence and revision triggers are defined.

## Outcome claim validation

- [ ] Evidence strength is assessed.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limits are reported.
- [ ] Missing stakeholder groups are documented.
- [ ] Alternative explanations are considered.
- [ ] Innovation claims are not made from adoption data alone.
