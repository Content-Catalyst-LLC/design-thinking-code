# Organizational Friction Protocol

## Purpose

Organizational friction analysis helps teams identify why promising innovations fail inside real institutions.

## Friction categories

1. **Silo friction:** teams or functions optimize separate goals.
2. **Decision latency:** slow or unclear approvals delay learning.
3. **Legacy-system constraint:** old technology or data architecture blocks implementation.
4. **Metric misalignment:** incentives reward behavior that conflicts with the innovation.
5. **Training gap:** users or employees lack the support needed to adopt the change.
6. **Ownership ambiguity:** no clear owner for adoption, maintenance, or improvement.
7. **Frontline workload:** operational teams absorb hidden labor.
8. **Governance gap:** accountability and review structures are weak.

## Review questions

- Which friction points are design problems?
- Which are governance problems?
- Which are resource problems?
- Which are technical-debt problems?
- Which are leadership or incentive problems?
- Which can be prototyped before scale?
