# Ethical Innovation Protocol

## Purpose

Ethical innovation review helps prevent design thinking from making existing systems more efficient without making them more just, accessible, or accountable.

## Core questions

1. Who benefits?
2. Who carries new burden?
3. Who is excluded?
4. Who was involved in the design?
5. What data is collected and why?
6. What decisions become automated or less contestable?
7. Does the innovation increase surveillance or control?
8. Does the innovation shift labor to frontline staff, users, or partners?
9. What harms would require pause or redesign?
10. Who has authority to revise or stop the innovation?

## Review domains

| Domain | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Accessibility | Disability, language, digital access, time constraints, and assisted pathways. |
| Equity | Distribution of benefits, burdens, risk, and exclusion. |
| Labor | Employee workload, discretion, dignity, surveillance, and burnout risk. |
| Privacy | Data minimization, consent, security, and purpose limitation. |
| AI governance | Transparency, bias monitoring, appeal, human review, and auditability. |
| Accountability | Named owner, escalation route, correction process, and review cadence. |
