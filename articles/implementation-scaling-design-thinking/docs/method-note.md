# Method Note: Implementation and Scaling Decision Support

This folder provides a transparent decision-support framework for implementation and scaling in design thinking.

The central model is:

\[
V_i = w_a A_i + w_o O_i + w_d D_i + w_g G_i + w_e E_i + w_f F_i - w_r R_i
\]

Where:

- **Adoption readiness** captures whether people are likely to understand, use, and normalize the intervention.
- **Operational fit** captures workflow, staffing, handoff, and support-system fit.
- **Durability** captures whether the intervention can survive time, turnover, maintenance, and routine use.
- **Governance readiness** captures ownership, accountability, decision rights, and escalation capacity.
- **Equity readiness** captures whether differential access, burden, and outcomes have been addressed.
- **Financial sustainability** captures funding, procurement, maintenance, and renewal capacity.
- **Composite risk** captures operational, governance, technical, equity, and financial risk.

## Recommended use

Use this model to support:

- implementation readiness review;
- scale-readiness assessment;
- rollout-stage evaluation;
- governance review;
- equity review;
- technical hardening prioritization;
- phased rollout decisions;
- stop, revise, pilot, scale, or retire decisions;
- post-deployment learning documentation.

## Interpretation

A high score is not scale approval. A strong candidate may still require governance approval, technical hardening, procurement review, accessibility testing, operational stress testing, equity monitoring, or additional stakeholder validation.
